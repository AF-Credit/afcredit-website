# Surface: Homepage (index.html) and site-wide design system (css/style.css, js/main.js)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a property investor with a deadline, or a broker with a case to place. Job: understand what AF Credit lends on, get an indicative cost, and start a quote or a call. Proof: real case studies and real fee figures. Constraints: retain navy #1b2b3b and coral #ff6a6b; references auracapital.co.uk, lovey.com and the supplied line-art house illustration; no kickers/eyebrows; no testimonials or review widgets; risk warning at the close.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A bridging lender that looks like a consumer product people are happy to use, not a private lender's brochure. The page is an illustrated high street: the property types AF Credit funds are the houses on it, and the first thing a visitor can do is price a loan. It refuses the category's dark-navy photo hero with a glass card and the "three equal cards then a table" scaffold.

OWN-WORLD: Sky ground #f3f7fb and white panels; navy #1b2b3b ink; coral #ff6a6b as the single action colour; illustration fills in pale blue #dbe7f3 and #b9cde3, foliage teal #2f7d6d, accent coral trees. Line-art SVG streetscape: 2px navy strokes, round joins, flat fills, outlined clouds. Manrope for display and body (700/800 display, 500/600 UI). Pill buttons, 22px panel radius, 1px navy-tinted borders, soft offset shadows 0 12px 32px rgba(27,43,59,.08). Coral selection, coral focus rings, themed scrollbar. No eyebrows: headings carry their own weight.

STORY: "This lender funds the property I'm looking at, will tell me the cost now, and I can start in one click, whether I'm the investor or the broker." They price a loan in the hero, see who it is for (two equal doors: investor, broker), explore products in a picker, read how the week runs, see three real deals with numbers, then act.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Light sky. Sticky white nav bar. Left 55%: headline "Bridging loans for the property other lenders won't touch." in two tones, two sentences of copy, actions "Get a quote" (coral) and "Place a case" (navy outline), then a line of four facts (0.79%, £10k–£2m, 75% LTV, 5–10 days). Right 45%: a white "What could it cost?" card: property value, cash needed, term, live illustrative rate and interest, button "Get a full quote" that carries the figures to get-a-quote. Across the full width beneath both, an illustrated street: terrace, auction lot with a board, shop with flat above, scaffolded refurb, clouds drifting; the calculator card overlaps the street's roofline. Primary action: the coral quote button in the hero copy and the card's button.

FORM: Illustrated consumer-fintech landing (pinned by brief: Aura Capital / Lovey). Position: brief-pinned, no roll. seed: none (unavailable).

Signature interaction: the hero calculator's rate and interest figures re-count as the sliders move, and the street's clouds drift continuously (paused under reduced motion). Secondary: the product picker switches panels with a short cross-fade.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
