Who we are

A direct lender built for
people who need to move fast.

AF Credit is a direct bridging lender. We lend our own capital and decide in-house, and we work with brokers and borrowers on the deals that need a fast, straight answer: auction deadlines, awkward titles, properties a mortgage lender won't touch.

Our story

Built for the deals
that can't wait.

Bridging exists to be fast. Too often it isn't, because a case passes through a sales desk, a case handler, an underwriter and a credit committee before anyone gives a real answer.

AF Credit was set up to take those layers out: a lender that decides for itself, talks to you directly, and completes quickly without cutting corners on due diligence.

We lend across England and Wales on residential, commercial and mixed-use property — from straightforward investment purchases to tenanted auction lots and complex commercial cases.

How decisions get made

One underwriter owns the case

1

Read by a decision-maker

The enquiry goes to an underwriter with authority to lend, not to a sales desk. If the security or the exit does not work, you hear that the same day rather than a fortnight later.

2

Valuation route set upfront

We decide at terms stage whether the case runs on AVM, desktop or a physical inspection, so the cost and the timetable are known before anyone spends money.

3

Same underwriter to completion

The person who agreed the terms runs the file through legals. No handover, and no second opinion arriving late to reprice the deal.

1

Direct principal lender

No-valuation capability

No valuation bridging is a large part of what we do. An AVM or desktop valuation removes the surveyor visit on eligible property, which can take weeks out of a deal.
