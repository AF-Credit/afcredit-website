The Challenge

Buying at auction demands speed and certainty. The investor secured a winning bid of £160,000 on a house in Dover, Kent, through a Modern Method of Auction, and needed to complete through their limited company within a 56-day window.

The auction platform asked for evidence of funding, so the investor approached AF Credit for a £120,000 auction bridging loan. Their priorities were clear: get the funding in place quickly, instruct one solicitor to handle both the purchase and the financing, and refinance onto a longer-term buy-to-let mortgage shortly after completion.

Fast Auction Funding with No Physical Valuation

We issued indicative terms on 13 July, based on a gross loan equal to 75% of the £160,000 purchase price. The case was assessed using an automated valuation model (AVM) instead of a physical valuation, which supported a quicker turnaround.

The following evening, at 10:31pm, the investor asked for written funding evidence to share with the auction platform within 24 hours. Once the limited company borrowing structure was confirmed the next morning, we updated the facility and security documents and supplied the final facility letter, setting out the proposed terms and figures, by 10:09am.

Alongside this, we coordinated the instruction of one solicitor firm to act on both the purchase and the financing, which helped streamline the process.

Clear Costs from the Outset

Certainty over costs mattered to the borrower. We issued a fully costed illustration at the start, explaining the loan charges and listing disbursements separately.

When the solicitor's fees came in higher than the figure in the original illustration, we reduced our arrangement fee to cover the difference. This kept the borrower's quoted overall costs for those items unchanged, with disbursements remaining separate.

Supporting the Purchase Through Legal Delays

While the funding response was quick, the vendor's legal pack was incomplete. We worked closely with the client and the solicitors to progress the purchase and resolve outstanding enquiries, stayed in regular contact, checked what was outstanding and followed up to keep the transaction moving.

Planning the Exit onto Term Finance

The borrower wanted to move onto a term mortgage as soon as possible after purchase, so we discussed the exit strategy during the legal process. When they raised concerns that Land Registry registration could hold up the refinance, we explained how evidence from the proposed term lender could support a request to expedite the application, and offered to help with that process to support an early exit.

The Outcome

For this investor, the bridging loan provided a practical route between an auction purchase and their intended longer-term finance, backed by an AVM-based assessment with no physical valuation, clear costs, responsive communication and a continued focus on progressing the transaction.
