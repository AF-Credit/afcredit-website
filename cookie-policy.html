Cookie Policy

What cookies this website uses, why, and how to control them.

Last updated: 21 July 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember preferences, and to provide analytics information to site owners. Similar technologies (such as scripts and pixels) are covered by this policy in the same way.

2. Cookies we use

Google Analytics data is aggregated and does not directly identify you. We do not use advertising, retargeting or social media tracking cookies, and we do not sell data collected through this website.

3. How to control cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings — including blocking all cookies or deleting existing ones. Instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

You can also opt out of Google Analytics across all websites with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Blocking analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use this website.
