How the quote works

Behind the numbers

Net advance. Enter what you want in your hand. The quote back-calculates the gross loan needed to deliver that net figure after all fees and retained interest are deducted — so there are no surprises on day one.

Rates. Rates are set by loan size and LTV. We interpolate linearly between the band floor and ceiling as LTV moves from 60% to 75% — the lower your LTV, the better your rate. Adverse credit may add a small premium.

Interest. Interest is retained — deducted upfront for the full term. You repay the gross loan in a single bullet at the end. No monthly payments.

Fees. Arrangement fee (2–3% of gross), legal fee (£1,600 estimated, dual representation) and admin fee (£995) are all deducted from the advance. No early repayment charge, subject to three-month minimum interest.

Common questions

Quote FAQs

Is this a loan offer?

No. These are indicative terms for illustration purposes only. A formal credit decision requires us to assess the property, exit strategy, and borrower profile. AF Credit issues same-day indicative terms on any enquiry, free and without obligation.

What does "net advance" mean?

The net advance is the amount that lands in your account on day one. It is the gross loan minus retained interest (deducted for the full term), minus the arrangement fee, legal fee and administration fee. This tool calculates backwards from the net advance you need so you can see the gross loan required.

Why does my credit profile affect the quote?

Adverse credit such as CCJs or defaults may attract a small rate premium and may limit the maximum LTV available — typically to 70% rather than 75%. All cases are assessed individually. If your profile is complex, contact us directly.

What is the minimum loan?

AF Credit lends from £26,000 to £2,000,000. The minimum net advance in this tool is £26,000.

Can I borrow against my home?

AF Credit provides unregulated bridging finance only. If the property being used as security is your main residence, your loan would fall under FCA regulation and AF Credit would not be able to assist. We lend against investment property, buy-to-let, commercial, semi-commercial, and uninhabited residential property.
