How the quote works

Behind the numbers

Net advance. Enter what you want in your hand. The quote back-calculates the gross loan needed to deliver that net figure after all fees and retained interest are deducted — so there are no surprises on day one.

Rates. Rates are set by loan size and LTV. The lower your LTV, the better your rate. Adverse credit may add a small premium to the base rate.

Interest. Interest is retained — deducted upfront for the full term. You repay the gross loan in a single bullet at the end. No monthly payments.

Fees. Arrangement fee (2–3% of gross), legal fee (£1,600 estimated, dual representation) and admin fee (£995) are all deducted from the advance. No early repayment charge, subject to three-month minimum interest.

Common questions

Quote FAQs

Is this a loan offer?

No. These are indicative terms for illustration only. A formal credit decision requires us to assess the property, exit strategy and borrower profile. AF Credit issues same-day indicative terms on any enquiry, free and without obligation.

What does "net advance" mean?

The net advance is the amount that lands in your account on day one. It is the gross loan minus retained interest (deducted for the full term), minus the arrangement fee, legal fee and administration fee. This tool calculates backwards from the net advance you need.

Why does my credit profile affect the quote?

Adverse credit such as CCJs or defaults may attract a small rate premium and limit the maximum LTV available — typically to 70% rather than 75%. All cases are assessed individually.

What is the minimum loan?

AF Credit lends from £26,000 to £2,000,000.

Can I borrow against my home?

AF Credit provides unregulated bridging finance only. If the property being used as security is your main residence, your loan would fall under FCA regulation and AF Credit would not be able to assist. We lend against investment property, buy-to-let, commercial, semi-commercial and uninhabited residential property.

Frequently Asked Questions

Bridging Loan Quote — Common Questions

How quickly can I get a bridging loan quote?
Our online tool gives you an instant indicative quote as soon as you submit the form — rates, fees and your estimated net advance are shown immediately. Once you submit your enquiry our underwriting team typically provides same-day credit-approved terms.
Is the quote a real offer, or just an estimate?
The online quote is indicative only — it shows you likely terms based on the information you enter. Formal credit-approved terms are issued by our underwriting team after reviewing your application and property details.
What bridging loan amounts do you offer?
AF Credit offers bridging loans from £26,000 up to £2,000,000 net advance across England and Wales.
What LTV can I borrow up to?
Up to 75% LTV on clean credit profiles. Up to 70% LTV for borrowers with adverse credit history including missed payments, CCJs or satisfied defaults.
Do I deal directly with AF Credit?
Yes. AF Credit is a direct principal lender — you deal with our in-house underwriting team from initial quote through to completion. There are no intermediary layers or third-party delays.
Do you offer bridging loans for auction purchases?
Yes. Our auction finance product is designed to complete within the standard 28-day auction deadline. We can provide same-day indicative terms and work with your solicitors to meet auction exchange and completion requirements.
Can I get a bridging loan with bad credit?
Yes. AF Credit lends to borrowers with adverse credit history including missed payments, satisfied CCJs and satisfied defaults. Cases involving IVAs are reviewed individually — contact us to discuss. Undischarged bankruptcy is not considered.
What properties do you lend on?
Residential (houses, flats, HMOs), commercial (offices, retail, industrial, mixed-use), semi-commercial, land with planning, and specialist properties including fire-damaged, derelict and uninhabitable assets. England and Wales only.
